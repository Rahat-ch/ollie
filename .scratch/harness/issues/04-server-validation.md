# 04: Only checked model output leaves the server

**Read first:** `CONTEXT.md` (capitalised terms are defined there); `.scratch/harness/spec.md`, Implementation Decisions › Phase 1 › Server-side validation; `.scratch/harness/decisions.md` #10; §4.7; ADR 0002's list of routes; `docs/research/langchain-harness.md` for background. Follow `AGENTS.md`. Deliver as one PR into `main`, merged before the next ticket starts. Never mention the former sponsor or the contest in anything you write.

**What to build:** The Coach route runs the Coach step itself (check, one retry with every reason, the Baseline). It returns only an accepted Coach output, a retried one, or the Baseline Plan, with where the Plan came from and any rejection reasons.
- It rebuilds what it checks against from the request: the known Problem IDs (this Session's evidence plus what the Notes already cite), and the Plan Space from the Knowledge Estimates (unlocks depend only on Mastery).
- The Summary route runs the Summary validator and its template fallback the same way.
- The device keeps its own check and fallback unchanged, so play still works offline.

## Comments

**2026-09-28, implementation.** Test first through the route seam: the new tests in `src/app/api/coach/route.test.ts` and `src/app/api/summary/route.test.ts` post a body to the real `POST` handlers with the Generation fake mocked in under `@/generation/anthropic`'s name, and read the response. Run against `main`'s two route files they fail 9 of 9 (the routes answered the model's output as it came); they were not committed red separately.

The Coach step is split so it can run on either side. `coachStep(generation, input, bounds, options)` in `src/coach/coach.ts` is the one ladder (check, one retry with every reason, the Baseline Plan), checked against `CoachBounds`: the known Problem IDs and the Profile the Plan Space comes from. `coachSession` gives it `sessionBounds(result, notes)`, as before, for the eval and the CLI. The route gives it `boundsFromInput(input)`: the evidence IDs plus the IDs the Notes already cite, and `profileFromInput` (the Knowledge Estimates, and the Session number as the count played), which is all the Plan Space and the Baseline read. `checkCoachOutput` now takes bounds rather than the Session. `serverCoachStep` (`src/coach/server.ts`) runs the ladder with the request's signal, shows the Coach the Plan Space the server rebuilt rather than the one the body carried, and strips the rejected outputs: the route answers `{ notes, plan, source: "coach" | "retry" | "baseline", rejections: [{ attempt, reasons, unavailable? }] }`. The deadline answer (`serverBaseline`) now has the same shape, one rejection marked unavailable, and `isServerBaseline` is gone. The Summary route runs `writeValidSummary` (which now takes the signal) through `serverSummary` and answers the Summary or the template with `source` and reasons only.

The device. `Coaching` is now two steps, `coach(result, notes)` and `summarise(input)`, not two model operations: `routeCoaching()` posts once to each route and hands the answer to `coachThroughServer` / `summaryThroughServer`, which check it again against the device's own Session (`acceptServerStep`, `acceptServerSummary`); `generationCoaching(generation)` runs the whole ladder locally and is what the tests use with the fake. When the route used the Baseline, the device plans its own from its Profile, keeps the Notes from before the Session and records the route's reasons, so the Notebook says "could not reach the Coach" for an unavailable one and "could not be used" for two rejections. An output the device's check refuses is rejected as the attempt it was and the device's Baseline is used. A route that cannot be asked (503, timeout, network) is one rejection and the Baseline or template at once, as before.

Tests: 9 route tests (accepted, retried, Baseline after two inventions, a Plan outside the Plan Space, a body claiming a wider Plan Space, a Hypothesis resting on an earlier Session's Problem; a Summary accepted, retried after an invented number, and the template), `src/coach/server.test.ts` (the Plan Space rebuilt equals the device's for six Sessions including the two that Master Unit 1 and Unit 2 and open the next Unit, the known IDs equal `knownProblemIds`, no retry after an abort, the signal reaches the call, and the device's check of each kind of answer), three `routeCoaching` tests in `coaching.test.ts`, and one browser test where the route answers a Baseline after two rejections: one request, recorded not unavailable, and the Notebook says the plan could not be used. Verified with `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` (661), `pnpm eval --fake` (run as `tsx src/cli/eval.ts --fake`; the regenerated report and charts not committed), `pnpm build`, and the whole browser suite (308) plus `e2e/coach.spec.ts` on `desktop-chrome` again after the new test (6 of 6).

Decided here, not in the spec: the device no longer retries through the route. The route has already run the retry, so a device retry would pay for up to two more calls; a 502 or a network failure now ends in the Baseline at once rather than a second request. The route's deadline covers the whole step, so a first call and a retry that together pass 75 s end as the deadline's Baseline, and the first rejection's reasons are not in that answer. A body whose Plan Space differs from the rebuilt one is not refused; the rebuilt one is used. The step is plain TypeScript in the route, not the LangGraph graph of ADR 0004, which is ticket 11; `coachStep` and `boundsFromInput` are what that graph's nodes can wrap.

# 02: The composition

**What to build:** The Session screen on an iPad reads as deliberate. Ollie stands 240 to 280 pixels tall at the bottom of the left column, directly under his speech bubble so the tail points at him, no longer pinned to the stage's corner; the number pad fills the height of its column through a row gap that grows with the viewport, with keys that stay 64 pixels and never pass about 72; the progress dots live in the stage's header row; the Repeat button stays beside the bubble and follows it into the portrait order; the asking, hint, correct, and reveal phases share one grid and only the bubble's words, the visual's marks, and Ollie's pose change; the new sizes are named tokens beside the existing ones. The design review page gains a grid of the Session screen in each visual (ten-frame, number line, Story) at each of the nine iPad sizes, taken by the review script, so the user can approve the composition against the canvas. Home, the Shop, the Parent Area, and the celebration are checked for overlap and sideways scroll at the same sizes, with any finding fixed if it is a one-line layout fix and otherwise recorded.

## Comments

**2026-09-17, implementation.** The stage's composition is now the canvas's, sized by tokens and measured at all nine iPad viewports in WebKit. Ollie stands in the Problem column under his own bubble, left-aligned with it so the tail points at him, at `--ollie-stage: clamp(240px, 32svh, 280px)`: measured **240px at 944x656 and 1133x744, 259 at 1080x810, 262 at 1180x820, 280 at 1366x1024** — inside the 240-to-280 budget at every landscape size, and `e2e/layout.spec.ts` now asserts all three of those things (under the bubble, left-aligned with it to within a pixel, 240 to 280) on every run. In portrait he steps up beside the bubble at `clamp(180px, 20svh, 240px)`, which is the order the spec asks for and is what leaves the stacked Equation, visual and pad their height. His rig, poses, mouth and Power animations are untouched: `Ollie.tsx` and `ollie.css` are not in this diff, and `src/app/globals.test.ts` is still green, because nothing animated was added or renamed.

**Height had to be found for him, and this is where it came from.** Under the bubble at 944x656 he did not fit: the column had 212px of slack and a Story's bubble ate most of it. Three tokens give it back without costing the Learner anything she has to count — `--stage-equation: clamp(44px, 7.5svh, 64px)` and `--stage-speech: clamp(18px, 2.6svh, 24px)`, so the Equation and Ollie's line shrink on a short screen and not on a tall one, and `--cell-max: clamp(44px, 7.2svh, 60px)`, which holds the ten-frame's cell at the design's 60px from about 830px of height up and lets it fall to 47px on the shortest iPad, still above Apple's 44pt floor. Measured cells: 60px at 1024 and 810 tall, 53 at 744, 47 at 656.

**The pad opens up rather than grows.** `--pad-key: clamp(64px, 6.6svh, 72px)`, `--pad-gap: clamp(8px, 1.4svh, 16px)`, `--pad-row-gap: clamp(12px, 2.6svh, 28px)`, so `NumberPad` lost its `w-88` and its fixed `gap-3`/`gap-2` and its width now follows its keys. Measured keys: 64px on every landscape iPad, 68 at 1366x1024, 71 to 72 in portrait; never under 64, never over 72, and the spec asserts both bounds. In portrait the same three tokens are re-declared against the viewport's **width** on `.session-pad`, because in one column the pad has to leave room for everything stacked above it; without that the last key ran 5px off the bottom at 810x1080 and 14px at 744x1133.

**The one acceptance point answered differently.** "The pad's height matches its column within a gutter at every landscape size" cannot be had at the same time as "keys between 64 and 72": five rows of 72px keys are 360px, and the body row on a 13-inch iPad is 936px, so matching it would need four 144px gaps, which is not a number pad — it is four keys and a lot of air, and a Learner's finger would have to travel the screen. The research says the same thing in §3 ("growing keys past ~72px is counter-productive"). What is built is the research's recommendation: the pad's key and both gaps grow with the screen, giving **365x375 at 1080x810 and 395x407 at 1366x1024** against 356 fixed before, and the pad is centred in its column so the air is split above and below it rather than pooling under it. That box is left unticked deliberately; if the user would rather have a pad that reaches, the lever is `--pad-row-gap`'s ceiling and nothing else moves.

**The review.** `scripts/design-review.mjs` gained `ipadScreens()`, which drives WebKit — not Chromium, because the device is an iPad and Safari's engine is what draws it there — through the three visuals at each of the nine sizes and writes `docs/design/screens/ipad-<w>x<h>-<visual>.png`; all 27 are committed and `docs/design/review-2026-09-17.html` shows them in a grid under the existing screens, with the composition to approve spelled out beside them. Every one was looked at. What the looking changed: the Story card, which was `size-56` fixed, is `--story-card: clamp(140px, 22svh, 280px)` so it is 280px on a 13-inch and 144 on the shortest iPad instead of a small tile in a void; the bubble is capped at 34em rather than 440px, which is what turned a Unit 3 Story from six lines into two or three and is most of why Ollie fits.

**The other screens, checked and one thing fixed.** `e2e/layout.spec.ts` now also drives Home, the Shop, the Parent Area and the celebration at all nine sizes, asserting for each screen's landmarks that nothing runs off either side, that `document.elementFromPoint` at the middle of each finds the thing itself rather than something drawn over it, and that the document never scrolls sideways. One real finding, fixed in one line: **Home's Path ran 120px off the right edge on every portrait iPad narrower than 864px** — its three 192px stops need 576px and the `pl-72` inset left only 456 at 744 — so the inset is now `pl-8 lg:pl-72`, which is unchanged at 1024 and above and clears Ollie and his bubble below it because the Path sits in the band above them. Nothing else was found: the Shop, the Parent Area and the celebration hold at all nine, and the Parent Area is meant to scroll down the page, so only its sideways edges are held to.

**Counts.** `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm build` clean. `pnpm test`: 574 of 575, the one failure `src/app/api/story/route.test.ts` pre-existing on a clean tree (the Pool ticket 10 filled now answers before the template). `pnpm test:e2e` across all eleven projects: **242 passed, 33 failed**, the 33 being three specs failing identically in all eleven — `unit3.spec.ts:29` and `shop.spec.ts:89` on the same `template`-versus-`pool` change, and `session.spec.ts:143` on a Skill renamed from "Teen numbers as 10 + n" to "Teen numbers". All three fail the same way on `git stash`ed HEAD in desktop Chrome; none is layout. `e2e/layout.spec.ts` itself is **88 of 88**. One more WebKit-only harness race was fixed in `powers.spec.ts`: Done goes home by a client navigation and the next Session's `goto` raced it, which WebKit resolves by cancelling one of the two, so the loop now waits for the home screen to land.

**Still unverified, as in ticket 01.** The base iPad's true landscape viewport and Safari's toolbar height cannot be measured here. The stage is `svh`-based and both readings of the base iPad are in the project list, so the layout is held to the shorter of each; `window.innerWidth` and `window.innerHeight` in Safari on the user's own iPad would settle them and would change nothing that is built.

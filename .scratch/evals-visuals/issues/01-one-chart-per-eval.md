# 01: One chart per eval score

**What to build:** `pnpm eval:chart` draws, from the latest eval report (or any report with `--report`), one small SVG per eval score beside the existing `convergence.svg`, each readable on its own in the demo video and the write-up, in the same hand-drawn paper style and design tokens as `convergence.svg`, with a subtitle naming which Coach ran (the fake or the model id) and the report's date: Evidence Integrity (the rate as the headline, with citations checked, unknown IDs, and inconsistent citations shown so 1.00 reads as "every citation real"); Detection (each planted child on a Session timeline, 1 to 20, with the Session the weakness was first named marked, or "never"); False positives (per child, supported Hypotheses against false ones, tuning and held-out marked); Plan sources (Coach, Coach after a retry, Baseline fallback, over all Sessions); Story validity (first attempt, within three attempts, template, and the Judge's gate: reported or withheld with the agreement fraction); Summary validity (the same shape). The write-up's eval section and the video plan link the charts, and `docs/evals/README.md` lists them. No new dependency; SVG assembled by code as `convergence.svg` is.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** resolved

- [x] Six SVGs are written next to `convergence.svg` from the latest report and regenerated by `pnpm eval:chart`, including from the fake report
- [x] Each chart reads on its own: a title, the subtitle with the Coach and the date, labelled axes or labelled marks, and no number that is not in the report
- [x] The charts use the design tokens and the paper style, and a test checks each SVG is well-formed and carries the report's numbers
- [x] The write-up, the video plan, and the evals README point at them

## Comments

**2026-09-17, implementation.** `pnpm eval:chart` now writes seven SVGs beside the report instead of one: `convergence.svg` as before, then `evidence-integrity.svg`, `detection.svg`, `false-positives.svg`, `plan-sources.svg`, `story-validity.svg`, and `summary-validity.svg`, all from a report file on disk and never from a live run, and `pnpm eval` regenerates all of them after a run the way it regenerated the chart. The drawing is in two new files with no new dependency: `src/evals/chart-kit.ts` holds what every chart is made of (the paper, ink, grid and track tokens; the mark colours; the shared font; XML escaping; a `paper()` frame that lays out the title, a subtitle wrapped to at most two lines, and the footer saying which report the numbers came from) and `src/evals/charts.ts` holds the six renderers and `EVAL_CHARTS`, the file-name-to-renderer list `writeCharts` walks. `src/evals/chart.ts` lost its private copies of the tokens, the font and the escape helper to the kit and is otherwise untouched, so `convergence.svg` is byte for byte what it was.

Each chart reads on its own. Evidence Integrity leads with the rate at 62px in leaf-deep, then the three counts it is made of (2,331 Problem citations checked, 0 citing an ID not in the Log, 0 disagreeing with their own claim) and a bar per split, so 1.00 is read as "every citation real" and not as a bare number. Detection is a Session timeline 1 to 20 per planted weakness, the Learner, the weakness and its split on the left, a mark at the Session a supported Hypothesis first named it, and "never named" in berry-deep at the right where the fake never names one; only the two Learners with a weakness planted are drawn, since the other four have nothing to detect. False positives is one bar per Learner of its distinct supported Hypotheses with the false ones marked off the end over a 2px paper gap, the split under each name, and the two splits' fractions along the bottom. Plan sources is a single stacked bar over all 120 Sessions the Coach planned, labelled inside, with Coach, Coach after a retry, and Baseline fallback each given its count and share underneath. The two validity charts share a shape: three bars over the sample (first attempt, within the bounded attempts, template fallbacks) and then the Judge's gate as its own panel — the score when it cleared calibration, "scores withheld" when it did not, with the agreement fraction, a meter, and the threshold marked on it. Every subtitle ends "Coach: the Generation fake. Report 2026-09-13." from `coach.generation` and `generatedAt`, and every number on every chart is one the report holds or a share of two of them.

Colour follows the `dataviz` method against the project's own tokens rather than a placeholder palette: leaf `#4CAF7A` with leaf-deep `#2F8A5B` for its text where a thing held, berry-deep `#B23F56` where it is the thing to worry about, and for plan sources the convergence chart's own Coach blue and Baseline orange with berry-deep for the retry between them. Both sets were run through the skill's validator on the paper surface: the plan-source trio passes every check, and leaf against berry-deep passes separation (ΔE 13.7 deutan, 29.9 normal) with a contrast warning on leaf that is answered by the direct label every bar carries. Leaf-deep against berry-deep, the first pairing tried, failed deutan separation at 2.4 and was dropped.

Tests are `src/evals/charts.test.ts`, run against the committed fake report `docs/evals/2026-09-13T17-38-41Z.json` rather than a fresh run: a small scanner checks each SVG is well-formed XML (tags balanced, attributes quoted, no stray markup), that it is on the paper token and under 12 KB, and that it names the Coach, the date, and the report file; then one test per chart asserts the report's own numbers appear as text, read out of the parsed report rather than typed in. `docs/evals/README.md` lists the seven charts and what each says, the write-up's "What the evals say" links all six beside the convergence chart, and the video plan's 2:25 shot now cuts to the integrity and plan-source charts on the two sentences that name their numbers. Verified with `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` (584 in 64 files), and `pnpm eval:chart`, whose six new SVGs (2.6 to 4.0 KB each) are committed; each was rendered to PNG through Playwright's Chromium and looked at, which is how the overlapping labels, the clipped titles and the subtitle running off the paper were found and fixed.

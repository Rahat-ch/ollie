# 14: Streak, Coins, and the Shop

**What to build:** The Learner earns ten Coins for every completed Session regardless of accuracy and spends them in a six-item Shop on a hat, an accessory, or a pet for the round Avatar with four colour bases. A Streak counts days with a completed Session by device local time, with two Freezes consumed automatically on a missed day and milestone animations at 3, 7, and 14 days. The Theme can be changed in the Shop. No leaderboards, lives, timers, XP, or purchasable currency.

**Blocked by:** 09 Onboarding and Parent Area

**Status:** resolved

- [x] Coins are awarded once per completed Session and never deducted
- [x] The Shop shows six items at 10, 30, and 70 Coins; buying equips the item on the Avatar and persists
- [x] A missed day consumes a Freeze if one is held and the Streak continues; with none held the Streak resets
- [x] Milestone animations fire once each at 3, 7, and 14
- [x] Changing the Theme in the Shop changes the Stories in the next Session

## Comments

**2026-09-13, implementation.** The rewards are one pure module, `src/rewards/`, with the Shop's catalogue beside the state transitions: `shop.ts` is six `AvatarItem` rows (id, name, slot, price) in price order — Party Hat and Stripy Scarf at 10, Round Glasses and Pet Snail at 30, Gold Crown and Pet Bunny at 70, across the hat, accessory, and pet slots the glossary gives the Avatar — and `rewards.ts` is the `Rewards` the Profile keeps (Coins, the Streak, the day of the last completed Session, the Freezes in hand, the milestones seen, the Items owned, and the one Item worn per slot) with three functions over it. `awardSession(rewards, sessionNumber, at)` is the whole of the earning: ten Coins (`COINS_PER_SESSION`) for a completed Session whatever the answers were, the Streak extended by the local day of `at`, and an `Award` for the celebration to show (the Coins paid, the Freezes spent, the milestone reached). `localDay` is the device's local time and never UTC (`getFullYear`/`getMonth`/`getDate`), so a Session at 23:00 belongs to that day and one at 00:10 to the next; `extendStreak` is a pure function of the previous state and the day, which is what makes the fixed-date tests possible. `buyItem` spends the Coins, keeps the Item, and wears it; `wearItem` puts an owned Item on or takes it off, one to a slot. Nothing in `src/loop` changed, and the Loop's `ProfileState` is untouched, so the Simulated Learners and the Baseline run exactly as before.

The Profile carries it: `Profile.rewards`, `PROFILE_VERSION` 4, and a version-gated migration like ticket 10's — a stored version 1, 2, or 3 Profile is carried forward with fresh rewards (its progress, identity, and Session intact), a version 4 Profile must carry rewards this version knows, and `isRewards` rejects an Item id or a worn slot the Shop does not sell, so a hand-edited store starts fresh rather than dressing the Avatar in something that does not exist. `beginPlay` reads the rewards onto the Play state and `celebrate` computes the Award from the Loop's `SessionResult`, so the celebration phase carries both; the Session screen writes the progress and the awarded rewards together when it celebrates.

The screens: `/shop` (`src/app/shop/`, guarded like `/play` and `/parent` — onboarding first) shows the Coins and the Streak, the Avatar in its colour wearing what it owns, the six Items as paper cards (Buy, Put on, Take off, or Keep playing when the Coins are short, with the card disabled), and the six Themes as a row that writes `identity.theme`; the Learner's way back is a paper Back to Ollie. The home screen gained the Streak and Coin chips beside the Avatar (which now wears its Items) and a paper Shop button opposite Play. The celebration gained a sun card with the Coins the Session paid and a leaf card with the days in a row, and, at a milestone, a plum card with paper rays that turn out of it once (`.milestone` in `globals.css`, the celebrate overshoot, off under `prefers-reduced-motion`). `src/ui/AvatarItems.tsx` holds each Item's picture once, drawn in the Avatar's own 80×80 frame so the Avatar wearing it and the Shop tile showing it are the same shapes, the tile cropping the frame with its own `viewBox`; `src/ui/RewardChips.tsx` is the Coin and the Streak mark and the two chips.

Readings to confirm. "Never deducted" is read as: nothing in play ever costs a Coin — no wrong answer, no Reveal, no missed day — and buying in the Shop is the only thing that takes one away, which is the spec's "spent only on Avatar Items". Freezes are granted nowhere in the spec, so the rule decided here is: the Learner starts with both (`MAX_FREEZES` 2) and a milestone hands one back, capped at two, which ties the grant to something the spec already names rather than inventing a cadence; the card says so ("A Freeze for the day you miss"). A missed day spends one Freeze per day missed; when the Freezes in hand cannot cover them all the Streak starts again at today and the Freezes are kept, since a Freeze that saved nothing is not spent. A second Session on the same day pays its ten Coins but does not move the Streak, and a Session pays its Coins once: `sessionsAwarded` holds the last Session number paid, so a Session that ended without its celebration and is finished on the next visit adds nothing. Milestones fire once each ever (`milestonesSeen`), so a Streak that resets and climbs back to 3 does not celebrate 3 again. The rewards live beside the Loop rather than inside `ProfileState`: the spec puts Streak and Coins behind the Loop seam, but the Loop has no clock and a Simulated Learner earns nothing, so `src/rewards` is pure data in and data out, imported by the Play reducer, and the Loop stays the math. The Theme change is the identity's, not the rewards', because that is where the Theme has lived since ticket 09.

Placeholders for ticket 15: the six Item pictures and the Avatar's own shapes are flat token-palette SVG in the right places and colours but not hand-tuned — the scarf in particular reads thin on its own tile — and the Streak's mark is a plain paper flame. The Avatar with an Item on renders for real, in the Shop, on the home screen, and after a reload.

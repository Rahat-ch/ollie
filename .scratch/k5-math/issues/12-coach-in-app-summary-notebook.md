# 12: Coach in the app, Parent Summary, and Ollie's Notebook

**What to build:** After a Session in the browser, the Coach runs through an API route, the Learner Notes and next Session Plan are stored with the Profile, and the next tap on Play builds that Session. The Parent opens the Parent Area and reads the Parent Summary written by Opus 5 from the Session Log and Notes: strategies practiced and Powers earned, evidence by Assistance State, what was Mastered, one bedtime activity, and never a claim about the Learner's thinking. Ollie's Notebook shows each Hypothesis as prose with its evidence as tappable Problems, what changed, and what is being tested next. The last seven Summaries are listed. Summary faithfulness joins the eval command with a Calibration Set of 10 Summaries.

**Blocked by:** 06 Coach in the Loop, 09 Onboarding and Parent Area

**Status:** resolved

- [x] Completing a Session triggers exactly one Coach run; if it fails, the next Session uses the Baseline Plan and the Notebook says so

## Comments

**2026-09-13, implementation.** The Coach reaches the app through two API routes and one record on the Profile. `src/coach/record.ts` is what the Coach leaves behind on the device: the Learner Notes, the next Session Plan, where that Plan came from (`coach`, `retry`, `baseline`) with every rejection reason, the number of the last Session coached, the Problems the Notes cite (id, Skill, the equation with its unknown blanked, Assistance State, Session number — the same view the Coach was given, pruned at each step to what the new Notes cite), what changed in the Notes after the last Session (`notesChanges`: a new Hypothesis, a status that moved, evidence that grew, a new strength), and the last seven Parent Summaries newest first (`addSummary`, `SUMMARIES_KEPT`). `Profile.coach` holds it, `PROFILE_VERSION` is 5 with a version-gated migration like tickets 10 and 14 (a stored version 4 Profile is carried forward with an empty record and keeps its progress, identity, rewards, and Session), and the stored record is checked by `CoachRecordSchema` in `src/coach/record-schema.ts`, which reuses the Coach's own `LearnerNotesSchema` and `SessionPlanSchema`, so a hand-edited store starts fresh rather than putting an invented Hypothesis in front of a Parent.

The run itself is `runCoaching` in `src/play/coaching.ts`, pure over a `Coaching` (the two Generation operations that run after a Session): `coachSession` from ticket 06 — its own retry-once-then-Baseline rule, not a second copy — then `applyCoachStep`, then `writeValidSummary`, then `addSummary`. `routeCoaching` is the browser's Coaching: `POST /api/coach` with exactly the `CoachInput` the engine builds (the Session's evidence, the Notes, the Knowledge Estimates, the Plan Space) and `POST /api/summary` with the `SummaryInput` (the Log tallied, what was Mastered, the Powers, the weakest Skill, the Notes); both bodies are zod-checked on the route against those shapes, which have no place for a Nickname, Avatar, or Theme, and a body carrying one is a 400 (ADR 0002, tested). Both routes run the Anthropic adapter when `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set and answer 503 with the reason at once when it is not, as `/api/story` does; a thrown call is a rejection like any other, so with no key the Coach falls back to the Baseline Plan with both reasons on the record and the Summary falls back to the template. `useSessionCoach` (`src/play/use-coach.ts`) fires it from the celebration phase, once per Session: a module-level set of Sessions started plus `lastSessionCoached` on the record, so a re-render, a reload, or a Session celebrated a second time starts nothing. Nothing on screen waits for it; the record is written when it lands. `nextPlan` in the Play reducer builds the next Session from the stored Plan when a Coach run stands behind the Session just played, and from the Baseline otherwise (including the Diagnostic Session, which the engine still recognises by identity).

The Parent Summary is the third Generation operation. `SummaryInput` is the Session Log tallied by Skill and Assistance State (`src/summary/summary.ts`), what was Mastered, the Powers earned, the weakest Skill practiced, and the Notes; `SummaryOutput` is two pieces of prose, `practiced` and `activity`. `src/summary/validate.ts` is the deterministic gate: every number in `practiced` must be one the input carried (its counts, the numbers inside the names and claims it holds, the lengths of its lists, and zero), and neither part may claim to know how the Learner was thinking (a closed list of phrases, word-bounded); `src/summary/write.ts` gives it two attempts and then `templateSummary`, the hand-written note over the same tally with a hand-written bedtime activity per Skill. `src/summary/prompt.ts` is the Opus 5 prompt, `src/generation/summary-schema.ts` the output schema and the route's input schema, and the adapter calls `messages.parse` on `claude-opus-5` at effort medium. The fake writes the tally in plain sentences, valid, so tests and `pnpm eval --fake` read as a Summary and not as the fallback.

The Parent Area's two placeholders are gone. `src/parent/notebook.ts` turns the record into Ollie's Notebook: each Hypothesis as a belief with its status, confidence, next test, and its evidence resolved to the actual Problems (with any cited Problem the device no longer holds named rather than quietly dropped); then the strengths, what changed after the last Session, what is being tested next (the Hypothesis under test), and, when the Coach was rejected twice, that the next Session is the Baseline's and why. `src/app/parent/Notebook.tsx` renders it with the evidence behind a 44 px control the Parent taps to see the Problems, each with its numbers, its Assistance State in the Parent's words, and its Session; `src/app/parent/Summaries.tsx` lists the last seven, newest first, each with the words Ollie wrote, the engine's evidence by Assistance State underneath them, what was Mastered, any Power, and the activity.

Evals: `src/evals/summaries.ts` writes the Summary the app would have written after each Simulated Learner's last Coach Session (six), scores validity with the Summary validator, and, through the Judge's second rubric, faithfulness — every claim checked against the evidence it was written from, failing an unsupported claim, a claim about thinking, or the three Assistance States run together. `calibrateJudge` is now generic over a Calibration Set and the `Judge` has `judgeSummary` beside `judgeStory`, both on Opus 5 in `judge-anthropic.ts`; `SUMMARY_CALIBRATION_SET` in `src/evals/calibration.ts` is 10 hand-labelled Summaries, 6 pass and 4 fail, every one of them accepted by the validator so the fails are only what a reader can see (a Hint-assisted success and a Revealed answer run together as "a little help", a claim about what she is picturing, a Mastery and a Power the evidence does not have, praise the evidence does not support with an activity for the wrong Skill). The fake Judge is the validator's opinion, agrees with 6 of 10 and so fails the 80% gate by design: `pnpm eval --fake` shows the gate withholding the score, exactly as the Story readability gate does. The validity computation Stories and Summaries share is `writtenValidity` in `src/evals/stats.ts`. `pnpm eval --fake` wrote `docs/evals/2026-09-13T16-47-49Z.json` with the new section and regenerated the chart.

Readings to confirm. The Summary is written from the Log *tallied* rather than from the Log itself, and the model returns words only, which is amendment 21: it is stricter than the spec's sentence and it is what makes "the Summary distinguishes first-try correct, Hint-assisted, and Revealed" true by construction, since the Parent Area renders the engine's counts under the prose. The numbers rule is applied to `practiced` and not to `activity`, because "count out ten spoons" is a suggestion and not a claim about the Session. The Summary says "your child" (amendment 22), the Notebook keeps the Nickname, which never leaves the browser. "Exactly one Coach run" is read as one run of the engine's rule, so two route calls when the first is rejected (amendment 23); the e2e asserts both that count and that nothing more is called on a reload. Powers do not exist yet: `runCoaching` takes the Powers earned and `useSessionCoach` passes an empty list with ticket 13 named in the comment, the `SummaryInput` carries them, the prompt asks for them by name, and the Summary card renders them, so ticket 13 has one call site to change. The Coach's retry is given the reasons; the Summary's second attempt is not, matching the Story writer, which a later ticket may want to change. A Session coached while offline keeps its Notes but loses its Problem IDs for citation later, as ticket 06 recorded. Vocabulary gaps for domain modelling: "record" (what the Coach leaves on the device), "belief" (a Hypothesis as the Parent reads it), "cited Problem", "practice row" (a Skill's evidence by Assistance State), "template Summary", and "faithfulness" as a score are used in code and here but are not in the glossary.
